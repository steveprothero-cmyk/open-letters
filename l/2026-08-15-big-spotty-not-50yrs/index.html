Letter to Cr Steele and Cr Kemp: the Big Spotty exclusion zone is 21, not 50, years old

I've written to Cr Brett Steele and Cr Denise Kemp, asking Steele to correct his claim that Big Spotty's protection zone was granted 50 years ago, by the next meeting on 25 August 2026.

To Brett Steele, Cr Denise Kemp · Copied to Cr Wilkins
